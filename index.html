Company Work & Client Projects

IDWP ka maqsad companies se digital projects lena aur verified clients/workers ko kaam ke mauke dena hai.

1. Company Project

Company project details, requirements, deadline aur payment terms IDWP ke saath confirm karti hai.

2. Client Work

Client available project ki requirements ke mutabiq kaam complete karke review ke liye submit karta hai.

3. Approval & Payment

Company kaam approve karke IDWP ko payment karti hai. Payment receive aur verify hone ke baad hi client payout process kiya ja sakta hai.

Project availability: Yahan live project list ya submission form tabhi active hoga jab project-management backend configure ho. Filhaal project approval aur payout automatic hone ka daawa nahi kiya ja raha.

IDWP Commission & Referral Earnings

IDWP ki commission har project ke pehle se tay percentage aur company agreement ke mutabiq hogi. Referral earnings sirf platform ke verified rules aur eligible transactions ke mutabiq hongi. Registration ya referral se income guaranteed nahi hai.

Company Advertising Services

Companies apne business/product ka promotion IDWP par video advertisement, banner advertisement, WhatsApp campaign ya email campaign ke zariye request kar sakti hain. Ads publish karne se pehle content, audience, price, schedule aur permission approve ki jayegi.

🎬 Video Advertising

Company apni ad video bhej sakti hai; approval ke baad website par video ad placement ke liye request kare.

🖼️ Banner Advertising

Company banner image aur destination link dekar banner placement ki request kar sakti hai.

💬 WhatsApp Advertising

Business ke liye WhatsApp promotional campaign ki details bhejein. Campaign tabhi chalega jab required consent aur approved WhatsApp setup ho.

✉️ Email Advertising

Company email campaign ki request kar sakti hai. Sirf consent-based, lawful mailing list use ki jayegi.

Advertising requests: Ye buttons IDWP Support ko request bhejne ke liye email draft kholte hain. Ad upload, scheduling, billing aur campaign analytics ko live karne ke liye alag backend/approval setup chahiye.
